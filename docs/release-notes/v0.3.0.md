## Wavelength v0.3.0: Listening tracker & stats

Wavelength now learns from how you listen.

### Listening tracker
Every time a song plays, Wavelength records how long you actually listened (paused time doesn't count) and classifies the listen:
- **Play:** you heard at least half the song, or 4 minutes
- **Skip:** you moved on within the first 30 seconds
- **Stopped partway:** anything in between
- **Replays** (repeat-one, or restarting a song with "previous") are counted too

Every song gets an **Affinity Score** from 0 to 100 (50 = neutral). It goes up with full plays, replays and favourites, and down with skips. Older listens slowly count less, so the score follows your taste as it changes.

### Stats screen
Open it with the 📈 button at the top of the library.
- **Overview** for 7 days, 30 days, 12 months or all time:
  - Listening time, plays, skips and skip rate, partial listens, songs heard, average share of each song heard, listening streak, never-played count
  - **Listening time per day/month** chart and a **When you listen** (hour of day) chart; tap a bar for exact numbers
  - **Top songs, top artists, top albums, top genres, most skipped**
  - **On your wavelength** (highest Affinity) and **Forgotten favourites** (loved, but not played in 30+ days)
- **All songs:** every song's plays, skips, partial listens, listen time, last played and Affinity, sortable by any of them, plus a "Never played" filter
- **History:** your last 300 listens, each marked played / skipped / stopped partway
- **Song stats:** tap any song in the stats screen, use **⋮ → Song stats**, or tap 📈 on Now Playing to see everything about one song, including its full listen history
- **Export** your listening history as a CSV spreadsheet, or **clear** it, from the ⋮ menu

### Favourites
- Tap ♥ on Now Playing, or **⋮ → Add to favourites** on any song
- A **Favourites** list sits at the top of the Playlists tab
- Favourites raise a song's Affinity Score

All listening data stays on your phone.

### Install
Download **Wavelength-v0.3.0.apk** below and open it. It installs over the previous version and keeps your playlists.
