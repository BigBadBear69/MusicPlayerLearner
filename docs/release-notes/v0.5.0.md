## Wavelength v0.5.0: Smart playlists & shuffle styles

### 🐞 Fixed
- **"Add songs" button hidden behind the player:** the mini player no longer covers the bottom bar when you're adding songs to a playlist (or in Settings), so the **Add** button is always reachable.

### 🎧 Made for you: playlists that build themselves
New **Made for you** section in the Playlists tab. These update automatically as you listen:
- **On Your Wavelength:** your highest Affinity Scores
- **Hidden Gems:** songs from artists, albums and genres you love that you've barely played
- **Rediscover:** favourites you haven't played in over a month
- **Never Played:** music on your phone you've never listened to
- **Morning / Afternoon / Evening / Late night Mix:** what you actually listen to at each time of day (the current one is listed first)
- **Skip Graveyard:** songs you skip almost every time

Play or shuffle any of them. To keep a copy, use **⋮ → Add to playlist…** at the top.

### 🔀 Shuffle styles
Choose how shuffle picks songs, from **Up next** (queue icon on Now Playing) or **Settings**:
- **Random:** every song has the same chance
- **Smart** (default): songs you love come up more, songs you skip come up less
- **Discovery:** songs you rarely play come up more

All styles keep the no-repeat rule and artist spacing. Changing style reshuffles the rest of the queue straight away.

**No-repeat window** is now adjustable in Settings (30–90%, default 70%).

### Install
Download **Wavelength-v0.5.0.apk** below and open it. It installs over the previous version and keeps all your data.
