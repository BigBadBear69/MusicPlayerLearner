## Wavelength v0.6.0: Equalizer, sleep timer, lyrics & widget

### 🎤 Lyrics
Tap **Lyrics** under the controls on Now Playing.
- **Synced lyrics** follow the song with the current line highlighted. Tap any line to jump to that part of the song.
- Wavelength looks for lyrics, in order:
  1. **inside the song file** (MP3, FLAC and M4A tags)
  2. a **lyrics folder** you choose in Settings, with `.lrc` files named like your songs
  3. **LRCLIB**, a free, open lyrics database (sends only the song's title, artist, album and length; can be turned off in Settings)
- Found lyrics are saved, so they load instantly next time.

### 🎚 Equalizer & bass boost
**⋮ → Equalizer** on Now Playing (or Settings → Equalizer & bass boost).
- 12 presets: Flat, Bass boost, Rock, Pop, Electronic, Hip-hop, Jazz, Classical, Vocal, Acoustic, Treble boost, Late night
- Fine-tune every band yourself, plus a bass boost slider
- Settings are remembered and applied every time music plays

### 🌙 Sleep timer
**⋮ → Sleep timer**: 5 minutes to 2 hours, or "end of this song". Music **fades out** over the last 10 seconds, then pauses. While a timer is running, a chip on Now Playing shows the time left; tap it to add 5 or 15 minutes or turn it off.

### 📱 Home-screen widget
Long-press your home screen → **Widgets → Wavelength**. It shows the cover, song and artist, with ⏮ ⏯ ⏭ buttons. Play works even when the app is closed: it continues where you left off.

### ⏩ Playback speed
**⋮ → Playback speed**, from 0.5× to 2×.

### 🎨 Now Playing looks
The Now Playing background is now tinted with the main colour of the album art.

### Install
Download **Wavelength-v0.6.0.apk** below and open it. It installs over the previous version and keeps all your data.
