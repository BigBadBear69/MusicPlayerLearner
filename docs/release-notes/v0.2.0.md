## Wavelength v0.2.0: Playlists & smart shuffle

This is the first version on the Releases page, so it includes everything from v0.1.0 too.

### New in 0.2.0
- **Playlists:** create, rename and delete playlists, and **drag the ☰ handle to reorder songs**. A Playlists tab sits one swipe to the left of Songs.
- **Song menu (⋮):** on every song: *Play next*, *Add to queue*, *Add to playlist*, *Go to album*, *Go to artist*. The ⋮ at the top of an album, artist, folder or genre acts on all of its songs.
- **Import and export .m3u playlists** to move playlists between Wavelength and other players.
- **Smart shuffle:**
  - When a shuffled playlist loops, **no song comes back until at least 70% of the playlist has played**
  - **Every loop is in a new order**
  - **Songs by the same artist aren't played back to back** when there's any alternative
- **Up next:** tap the queue icon on the Now Playing screen to see what's coming in the real play order. Tap a song to jump to it, or ✕ to remove it.
- **Wavelength remembers your queue:** reopen the app, or press play on Bluetooth headphones after a restart, and it continues where you left off.

### Included from 0.1.0
- Automatic music scan (MP3, FLAC, M4A, OGG, OPUS, WAV); Songs, Albums, Artists, Folders and Genres tabs; search
- Background playback with notification and lock-screen controls, plus headphone, Bluetooth and car buttons
- Now Playing screen with shuffle and repeat (off / all / one)

### Install
1. Download **Wavelength-v0.2.0.apk** below on your phone.
2. Open it. If asked, allow your browser to "install unknown apps".
3. Open Wavelength and allow access to your music.

Future versions install over this one and keep your playlists.
