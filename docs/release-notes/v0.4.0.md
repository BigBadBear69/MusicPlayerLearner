## Wavelength v0.4.0: Discover & multi-select playlists

### ✨ Discover: new songs similar to the ones you love
Open it with the ✨ button at the top of the library.
- Wavelength picks the songs you love (high Affinity Score, favourites, songs you've thumbed up) and finds similar music you **don't already have**.
- **Top picks for you**, **Because you like [artist]** and **New artists for you** (artists not in your library yet).
- Songs that several of your favourites point to rank higher. Artists you usually skip are pushed down. Anything already on your phone is left out.
- **▶ 30-second previews** right inside the app (tap the cover). How long you listen counts as feedback too.
- **👍** = more like this (it becomes a starting point for future picks) · **👎** = hide it, and see less of that artist
- **🔖 Wishlist:** save songs for later. When a saved song shows up on your phone, the Wishlist marks it and you can play it from there.
- **Listen on…** opens the full song in YouTube Music, Spotify, YouTube or Deezer.
- Recommendations refresh automatically every few days, or tap ⟳.

**Better matches with a free Last.fm key:** go to **⋮ → Settings**, tap *Open Last.fm*, create an API key (about 2 minutes), paste it and tap **Test & save**. Without a key, Discover still works using Deezer's related artists.

Only song and artist names are sent to Last.fm and Deezer. You can switch Discover's internet use off in Settings.

### ➕ Add many songs to a playlist at once
Open a playlist and tap **Add songs**: tick as many songs as you like, then add them all in one go.
- Search, plus filters: All songs, Not in playlist, Favourites, Recently added
- **Select all** picks everything shown
- Songs already in the playlist are marked ✓

### Also new
- **Settings** screen (⋮ → Settings): Last.fm key, Discover on/off, clear Discover cache, rescan music.

### Install
Download **Wavelength-v0.4.0.apk** below and open it. It installs over the previous version and keeps your stats and playlists.
