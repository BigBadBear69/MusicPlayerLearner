## Wavelength v0.1.0: Foundation

The first build of Wavelength: a music player for the songs already on your phone.

### What's in it
- **Automatic music scan:** finds every song on your phone (MP3, FLAC, M4A, OGG, OPUS, WAV) and rescans when you add or delete files. Ringtones, notification sounds and clips under 20 seconds are ignored.
- **Browse:** Songs, Albums, Artists, Folders and Genres tabs, with cover art.
- **Search** across songs, albums and artists.
- **Playback:** background play, notification and lock-screen controls, headphone, Bluetooth and car buttons; pauses automatically when headphones are unplugged.
- **Now Playing screen:** large cover art, seek bar, shuffle and repeat (off / all / one).
- Play or shuffle any album, artist, folder, genre or your whole library.

### Install
1. Download **Wavelength-v0.1.0.apk** below on your phone.
2. Open it. If asked, allow your browser to "install unknown apps".
3. Open Wavelength and allow access to your music.
